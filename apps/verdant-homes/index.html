Our Story

A Legacy of
Trusted Service

Verdant Homes was founded in 2002 by Charlotte Reeves, a former architect who believed the real estate process deserved far more care and discretion than it was typically afforded. Twenty-two years later, that belief still drives everything we do.

We work exclusively with a select roster of clients — never at volume — so that every transaction receives the meticulous attention it deserves. Our advisors average eighteen years of experience and bring a level of market knowledge that is simply unmatched.
